## Scope of status labels

Updated for beta.22. A corrected or mitigated row records a bounded implementation control and its regression evidence; it is not independent acceptance of the whole system. The beta.18–22 targeted reviews were performed with AI assistance. Independent statistical, security and deployment acceptance remain pending.

The owner actions in R-001, R-002, R-003 and R-024 remain open. This update does not approve public release, production use, asset redistribution or a general schema-v7 upgrade. R-013 now has an implemented credential/session correction with targeted developer regressions; this does not close independent security acceptance.
